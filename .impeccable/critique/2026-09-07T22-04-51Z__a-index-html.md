## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored, not generic — but the authorship is concentrated in one component, and the composition wrapped around it is category-interchangeable.

The Finder window earns the verdict alone. It is not an illustration of the product, it IS the product's argument: rows of real filenames with real extension chips, answering the buyer's actual objection ("am I paying R$47,99 for a generic PDF?") with a falsifiable object instead of a sentence. Remove it and a template remains: #para-quem is four Netflix hover-zoom tiles with no relationship to sanitary documentation, and they lie — divs with cursor:pointer that do nothing. ## Overall Impression

The page has one genuinely excellent idea and does not trust it. "Here is the actual paper" is a strong, product-specific argument that no competitor can copy — and from #para-quem onward the page stops using it and reaches for the launch-page kit instead. Meanwhile the proof itself does not survive counting: the tab label says 14 and renders 23, the tabs total 53 against a promised 54, and the meta description claims "+54".

The single biggest opportunity: move Ana above the price and put the guarantee inside the price card. Today the two facts that close all three buyer types — a 7-day refund and an author who used to be on the inspector's side — both sit on the far side of the ask.

Notably clean: no promise of guaranteed approval anywhere. Every headline, CTA, badge and microcopy was checked against the PRODUCT.md hard rule. "GARANTIR MEU KIT" is secure-the-kit, not guaranteed-approval; "pronto para a vistoria" is readiness, not outcome. No P0.

## What's Working

1. The Finder as proof-before-purchase. It converts an unverifiable claim into a verifiable object, and it works because it is falsifiable — a buyer holding a fiscal's list can check it row by row. Stronger than any testimonial, which matters because PRODUCT.md confirms none exist and none may be invented.
2. Playfair discipline held, verified at exactly three. Computed styles show serif on "R$ 9", "Ana Roberta Ribeiro" and "Perguntas frequentes" — the system's own rule, kept. The serif lands only where a human or a number needs warmth, so the Inter/navy mass reads institutional rather than cold.
3. The threat is calibrated honestly. "pode gerar penalidades, como multa" — modal verb, no invented figure, no countdown, no "últimas vagas", and the red highlight animates in once. For a product whose equity is an ex-inspector's credibility, that restraint is the strategy.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The headline is empty for ~2 seconds, then never resolves into a sentence
What: the typing script fires setTimeout(type, 1800) after DOMContentLoaded (measured 164ms), so .hero__line1 reads "Seu |" — no noun — for the first ~2s of a paid visit, then erases and retypes forever. The assembled line "Seu restaurante com uma pasta pronta em minutos." has no verb; land mid-cycle and you read "Seu delive| com uma pasta pronta em minutos." B's detector independently flagged blinking-cursor in the first viewport.
Why it matters: the most expensive pixels on a paid landing page are the headline at first paint, and they are blank. Compounding it, five elements compute animation-iteration-count:infinite at rest plus the Finder auto-scroll and the typing loop, and style.css contains no prefers-reduced-motion block at all — against the system's own rule, "o pulso pertence a um elemento por página."
Fix: server-render "Seu restaurante" into #typed-text so the H1 is whole at first paint and animate only the second word. Rewrite as a sentence: "Seu restaurante pronto para a vistoria em minutos." Keep one infinite animation (the price CTA), drop the rest, and add @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce).
Suggested command: /impeccable animate

### [P1] The inventory contradicts itself — "Planilhas (14)" renders 23 rows, and the tabs total 53, not 54
What: verified live. The tab is labelled "Planilhas (14)"; #tab2 contains 23 <li>. Tabs render 11+23+7+12 = 53. The page states the count four ways: "+54 documentos" (meta), "Mais de 50 documentos" (subtitle), "Todos os 54 arquivos" (price list), "+30 Planilhas e Checklists". PRODUCT.md records exactly 54, audited.
Why it matters: DESIGN.md states it directly — "Falsificar o conteúdo dessa janela quebra a única prova que a página oferece." A compliance buyer is precisely the person who counts. "+54" also asserts more than 54, which is untrue.
Fix: correct the tab label, reconcile the 53 rendered rows against the audited 54, and make every number on the page say 54 without the plus sign.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] The decision point has no reassurance — the guarantee is 2.7 viewports below the buy button
What: .price-v3-card offers two seals, "Compra 100% Segura" and "Acesso Imediato" — both true of every page on the internet. The 7-day guarantee first appears in FAQ item 5 at y=9307 on mobile, 2162px below .price-v3-btn at y=7145. #sobre (Ana) also sits below #preco.
Why it matters: this is the highest-anxiety moment for all three buyer triggers, and the two facts that would close them are on the far side of the ask. PRODUCT.md says Ana's authority is "o eixo da página, não um bloco de rodapé", and authority-from-inside-the-inspection is the one claim a competitor cannot truthfully copy — spent after the money question.
Fix: put "7 dias de garantia — reembolso direto pela Hotmart" inside .price-v3-card. Move #sobre above #preco so the order reads proof → author → price. Replace "Compra 100% Segura" with a claim not true of every website.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout

### [P1] FAQ #3 answers "Sim." to a question PRODUCT.md requires qualifying
What: "Esses documentos atendem à legislação vigente?" → "Sim. Os documentos foram elaborados com base na RDC ANVISA nº 216/2004…". PRODUCT.md constrains: the base is federal, and municipal/state requirements vary and are not covered. That limit appears nowhere on the page. (The "não indicado para indústria de alimentos" exclusion is correctly present — that half is right.)
Why it matters: it is the refund generator, and the same class of promise-boundary problem as the approval rule, one step removed. For a product whose equity is an ex-inspector's credibility, an overclaim about legal coverage costs more than the refund does.
Fix: append "O material cobre a RDC 216/2004, que é federal. Exigências municipais e estaduais variam — confira com a VISA do seu município." Repeat near the price card.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

### [P2] The accessibility floor is below the system's own documentation
What: white on #E63B2E measures 4.2:1 against the 4.5:1 minimum — the alert-red token itself fails WCAG AA, and .price-v3-badge uses it as a background. style.css contains no :focus or :focus-visible rule at all; its only outline declaration is html, body { outline: none }, so keyboard users fall back to the UA ring on a lilac button over navy — while DESIGN.md documents a "Hover / Focus" treatment that was never implemented. .sticky-cta__btn is tabbable inside a container marked aria-hidden="true". Collapsed .faq-answer uses max-height:0 only, so all six answers are announced regardless of aria-expanded. The footer's CNPJ/address line computes to ~3.0:1.
Why it matters: red is the urgency signal on a page selling regulatory compliance, and it is the one colour that fails the standard. The focus gap makes the whole purchase path unusable-by-feel for keyboard users.
Fix: darken alert-red until white clears 4.5:1, or stop using it as a background behind white text. Implement the documented focus ring. Add hidden to collapsed answers, remove the tabbable button from the aria-hidden container, and lift the footer text above 4.5:1.
Suggested command: /impeccable audit
